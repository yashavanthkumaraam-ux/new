About JeevaDrop ❤️

Every Drop Creates Hope

JeevaDrop is a blood donation and emergency blood availability platform created with one meaningful mission: to connect blood donors, hospitals, and people in need of blood.

In medical emergencies, finding the right blood group at the right time can be challenging. JeevaDrop aims to make this process easier by helping people discover potential blood donors, check available blood bank stock, and communicate their blood requirements through one convenient platform.

Our Mission

To make blood donor discovery easier, improve access to blood availability information, and encourage more people to become voluntary blood donors.

Our Vision

A world where no one struggles to find a potential blood donor because of a lack of information or connections.

What We Offer

  • Find Blood Donors: Search for potential donors by blood group and location.
  • Blood Bank Information: Explore registered blood banks and their available stock information.
  • Emergency Blood Requests: Share urgent blood requirements to help connect patients with potential donors.
  • Donor Registration: Register as a donor and become part of a lifesaving community. Donors show as available again 90 days after their last donation.
  • Hospital Access: A dedicated login for authorized hospitals to update the stock and details of their own blood bank.
